HARPED

Midnight at the Conservatoire. You stayed late to practise. The Academy's prize gilded pedal harp — magnificent, temperamental, hopelessly vain — has rolled out of the recital hall and is prowling the practice-room corridors, hunting for an audience.

The trick, dear: hold PERFORM (SPACE, or the button) — Huw Boucher's Fauré Impromptu swells, and the vain harp freezes to listen. It can't move while something that gorgeous plays.

Performing burns propranolol (your nerve); rest to refill. Find Skaila Kanga for the tuning key, then reach the recital-hall door. Wander the studios — there are mementos to find.
